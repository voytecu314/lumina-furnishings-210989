THE PRACTICAL DETAILS

Good questions,
straight answers.

Wondering about a material, delivery, or how to care for your new piece? We're happy to help.

Ask us directly →
Where is Lumina furniture made?+

Our pieces are designed by our studio and made in small runs with skilled craft partners. Get in touch for the current maker and origin details for a particular design.

What wood and finishes do you use?+

We select solid hardwoods such as oak and ash, and use durable, carefully considered finishes. Exact timber and finish options vary by design; we're glad to share specifics before you order.

How long will my order take?+

Many pieces are made to order. Lead times depend on the design and current workshop schedule, so we'll confirm a realistic delivery window with your quote.

Can I request a custom size or repair?

Often, yes. Some designs can be made to different dimensions, and we can advise on repairs and care. Send us a note with what you have in mind.

How should I care for my furniture?+

Keep wood away from prolonged moisture and direct heat, and use a soft, slightly damp cloth for everyday cleaning. We'll include tailored care guidance with your piece.
